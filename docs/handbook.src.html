Studio handbook

Running Mizuki Flora bookings

Everything your booking system does, and how to do it. No technical knowledge needed — every section starts by naming the screen you should be on and what it is for.

Sections are numbered so you can say "section 6" when you need a hand with something. Keep this page bookmarked.

01Start here

The two halves of the system, and which one is yours.

There are two sides to this. Students use the booking page on your website. You use the studio console — a separate, private site that only you and your team can open.

WhereWho uses itWhat happens there
mizuki.com.sg/book-a-class Students See your classes, book a place, change or cancel a booking
api.mizuki.com.sg/admin You and your team Everything else — classes, students, payments, emails

The one rule worth knowing

A class can never be overbooked by students. When someone takes the last place, that place is gone the instant they take it — even if another person is halfway through booking it at the same moment. You never have to check for this.

Two things on every screen

  • Quick add, top right — add a class or a student from wherever you are.
  • Search, at the top of the menu — find any student by name, email or phone.
Times

Every time shown anywhere in the system is Singapore time, no matter where you or your students happen to be.

02Signing in

Screen: the sign-in page. Getting into the console.

Tap the eye icon in the password box to check what you typed before signing in.

If you forget your password

  1. Click Forgot your password? underneath the Sign in button.
  2. Type the email address you sign in with, and click Send me a reset link.
  3. Open the email and follow the link to choose a new password.
If that email does not arrive

Check your spam folder first. If it is genuinely not there, anyone else on your team can send you a fresh link from the Team page — see section 12.

Staying signed in

You stay signed in on your own devices, so you will not be typing your password every day. Use Sign out at the bottom of the left-hand menu if you are on a shared or borrowed computer.

Changing your password later

Settings → Security has Change password, and Set up two-factor sign-in if you want a code from your phone required as well as your password.

03Your dashboard

Screen: Dashboard. What needs you today.

Things that need you today

The box at the top is the only part you must read. It lists anything waiting on a decision from you — a payment to check, a class that is overbooked, a course nobody can buy yet. When this box is empty, nothing needs you.

Each item is clickable and takes you straight to the screen where you deal with it.

The four numbers

NumberWhat it counts
Classes todayClasses you are teaching today, and how many students to expect
Students · next 7 daysPeople booked into the coming week, and how many places are still free
Bookings this monthPlaces taken this month, confirmed and held together
Students on fileEveryone who has ever booked with you

Further down

  • Today's classes — your teaching list for the day.
  • How full is the week — one circle for the next seven days, booked against free.
  • Next classes — what is coming, and how full each one is.
  • Course packages running low — students nearly out of sessions, so you can mention it before they run out.

04The calendar

Screen: Calendar. Every class you are running.

Reading a class at a glance

The small coloured bar at the left of each line tells you the course — the key along the bottom of the calendar shows which colour is which. Use All courses at the top right to show just one course at a time.

The number on the right is how full a class is. Its colour is the part to notice:

Looks likeMeans
No number at allNobody has booked yet
Blue 3/8Filling up normally
Amber 7/8Two places or fewer left
Grey 8/8Full — students see it as full and cannot book
Red 9!More people booked than places. Needs you.

Moving a class to another day

Drag it. Pick the class up, drop it on a different date, and the system works out who is affected before anything changes.

If students are booked in, you get a confirmation box telling you how many, and asking whether to email them about the change. Nothing moves until you say yes.

Adding a class

  1. Click + Add class at the top right.
  2. Choose the course, the date and the start time.
  3. Set how long it runs and how many places there are.
  4. Add it. It appears on the calendar and on your website straight away.
Your weekly timetable is automatic

Your regular weekly classes are created for the next three months on their own, so you only add classes by hand for one-off workshops and weekend courses.

On your phone

A month grid does not fit on a phone, so the calendar opens as a list of the next fortnight instead — full class names, times and how many are booked. Tap month to switch to the grid if you need it.

Screen: Calendar, then click any class. Who is coming, and everything about that class.

The four numbers at the top

Held backPlaces you have kept off the website, to fill yourself
Class sizeTotal places in the room

Changing the class size

Use − and + under Class size. This changes this one class only, not every class of that course.

Keeping places for chat bookings

This is the one to know about. If someone asks for a place over WhatsApp or in person and you want to keep space for them, use + under Held back for chat bookings.

Held-back places disappear from your website immediately — students cannot take them — but they stay yours to fill by hand. A class with 8 places and 2 held back shows 6 available online.

Adding a student yourself

  1. Click + Add student.
  2. Search for them if they have booked before, or type their details if they are new.
  3. They join the list, and get the same confirmation email as an online booking.

Marking who turned up

Each person on the list has a tick to mark them as attended, and Remove to take them out of the class. If someone used a course session and did not come, removing them returns that session to their package automatically.

Cancelling a class

Cancel class at the bottom. You are shown exactly who is booked and asked whether to email them. Anyone who used a course session gets it back.

You can undo this

A cancelled class can be brought back for 30 days, and the students who were booked come back with it — except anyone who had already cancelled themselves, who correctly stays out.

06Confirming payments

Screen: Payments. Places waiting for you to say yes.

This is your daily job. Two different situations land on this screen, and the line under each person's name tells you which:

The line saysWhat to do
paid, shop order #5241 They have paid through your shop. Check the money arrived, then confirm.
no payment yet — arrange it before confirming They asked to join but have no course package. Sort payment out with them first.

Confirming a place

  1. Check the payment however you normally would — your bank, or your shop's orders.
  2. Click Confirm place.
  3. The student is emailed their confirmation immediately, with a calendar invitation attached.

If the payment never came

Click No payment. You are asked for a reason, and that reason goes into the email to the student — so tell them something useful. Their place goes back on sale straight away.

Their place is safe while you decide

Nobody else can take it. There is no time limit and nothing expires — a place waiting here stays held until you confirm it or release it.

Checking what you already did

The History tab lists everything you have confirmed or declined, most recent first — so you can answer "did I already deal with Sofia?" without hunting.

07Students

Screen: Students. Everyone who has booked, and their course packages.

Why the reference numbers

Two students can genuinely have the same name. The reference — MZ-0023 — is what tells them apart, and it is short enough to say out loud on the phone.

Opening a student

TabShows
OverviewAttendance, courses taken, what they have booked next
BookingsEvery class they have ever booked
PackagesCourse sessions bought, used and left — and where to add more
DetailsContact details, and your private notes about them

Course packages

IFDA and Preserved Flower are sold as a block of sessions. Booking a class uses one; cancelling gives it back. You never have to count anything yourself.

On the Packages tab you can give a student more sessions, or extend how long they have to use them. Every change is recorded with who made it and when.

Adding a student by hand

+ Add student at the top right — for someone who booked over WhatsApp or walked in. Name, email and phone is all you need.

Duplicate accounts

If the same person books twice with slightly different details — a typo in their email, say — a Possible duplicate accounts panel appears at the top of this page.

Click Join into the one above and their bookings and course sessions move onto a single account. Nothing is deleted.

Mostly prevented already

When someone tries to book with an email or phone number you already have on file, they are asked to sign in instead of making a second account. This panel is for the ones that slipped through before.

08Days you are away

Screen: Closed dates. Closing the studio for a day or a fortnight.

  1. Choose the first and last day you are closed.
  2. Add a short reason for yourself.
  3. Click Check what this affects — nothing has changed yet.
  4. You are shown every class in that range and every student booked into them.
  5. Then decide: cancel those classes and email everyone, or close only the empty ones.

Closed days vanish from your website the moment you save, so nobody can book into them.

Always look at the preview

The preview exists so you never cancel a class without knowing who was in it. It costs one click, and it is the difference between closing a quiet week and cancelling on eleven people by accident.

Changing the wording

  1. Choose the message you want to change from the list on the left.
  2. Edit it. The preview on the right updates as you type.
  3. Click Save changes.
  4. Click Send test to me to see it arrive in your own inbox.
Which box to edit

Subject is plain text — change it freely. Email body is written in web code, so change the words and leave the bits inside angle brackets alone. Watch the preview as you go: if it still looks right, you are fine.

The chips marked studentName, sessionDate…

These fill themselves in for each student. Click one to drop it into your text. Leave them as they are and every person gets their own name, class and date.

Nothing here you can break

Reset to original puts any message back exactly as it started.

10How you are told

Where booking alerts reach you, and why there are three places.

When something happens you are told in up to three ways at once. Three, because email alone gets missed.

WhereGood for
The bell in the consoleAlways there, never lost — the reliable one
EmailA record you can search and forward
TelegramYour phone buzzing within seconds

The bell

The red strip at the top of the panel is for payments waiting on you. That count does not disappear when you look at it — it clears when the work is actually done.

The email you get

These go to everyone on your team, plus any extra addresses you add on the Team page. You also get one when a payment needs checking, and a short summary each morning.

Telegram on your phone

  1. Open Telegram and find your studio bot.
  2. Press Start. This step is required — a bot cannot message you until you have opened a conversation with it.
  3. In Settings → Connected services, click Find my chat and pick your chat.
  4. Click Send a test message to prove it works.

Where all of this is set up

SettingWhat it is
Send fromThe address students see your emails come from
Replies go toWhere a student's reply lands
Resend API keyWhat actually posts the email. Already set up.
Telegram bot token and chat IDOptional — alerts on your phone
If emails stop arriving

Your dashboard will say so, and this panel names both the problem and the fix. If it mentions older messages that failed before a setting was corrected, Clear these dismisses that count once you are happy.

Set your real class sizes

Courses start out with a placeholder of 8 places. Change these to your real numbers before students start booking, or you will end up with classes sized wrongly.

Changing it here affects classes created from now on. To change one that already exists, open it on the calendar — see section 5.

Adding a course

  1. Click + Add a course under the table.
  2. Give it a name, and choose how it is paid for.
  3. If it is sold in your shop, paste the product ID from WooCommerce → Products.
  4. Set the class size, and how much notice students need to change a booking.
  5. Pick a colour — that is how it appears on your calendar.

Set courses

Underneath is Set courses — courses booked as a whole run rather than one date at a time, like the Autumn Ikebana Course. One purchase books every date in the run, and each date is listed with how many places are left in it.

Retiring a course

Use Archive. It disappears from your website, but every past class and student record stays intact. Restore brings it back.

12Your team

Screen: Team. Who can sign in, and where booking alerts go.

Adding someone

  1. Type their name and email at the bottom, and click + Add admin.
  2. You are given a link. Copy it and send it to them however you like.
  3. They open it, choose their own password, and they are in.

The link works once and expires after 72 hours. If they miss it, click New link to issue another.

Why a link and not a password

A password sent by email sits in two inboxes forever. A link that expires and works once does not — and they choose their own password at the end of it.

Helping a colleague who is locked out

Reset password next to their name sends them a fresh link. This is also how you get back in yourself if your own reset email never arrives — ask someone else on the team to do it for you.

Removing someone

Remove access signs them out everywhere immediately. Their record stays, so anything they did is still accounted for. You cannot remove your own access — ask a colleague to.

Where booking alerts go

Everyone on this page is emailed automatically. Use Edit extra addresses to add anyone else who should hear about bookings without needing to sign in.

13What students see

Your website. Worth knowing, so you can talk someone through it on the phone.

On mizuki.com.sg/book-a-class there are two tabs: Book a class and My bookings.

Booking

  1. They see three months of classes, with dates that have classes highlighted.
  2. They click a date and see every class that day — time, length, places left. Full classes are marked and cannot be clicked.
  3. They click a class, fill in their details, and book.

If they are already signed in, the form asks only for a message — their name, email and phone come from their account, so their details can never drift.

Booking for someone else

A parent booking for a child ticks This place is for someone else and gives that person's name. Your class list shows the child; the booking stays on the parent's account.

My bookings

Students sign in with a link emailed to them — there is no password to forget. They can see what they have booked, how many course sessions they have left, change a booking, cancel one, and download a calendar invitation.

Changing a booking

Each booking shows its own deadline in plain words — "You can change this until Wed 19 Aug, 10:00 AM". After that the button stops working and they need to call you. You can always move someone yourself, whatever the deadline says.

14Common questions

The things most likely to come up.

A student says they did not get their confirmation

Open them on the Students page and check the email address is right — a typo is the usual cause. Ask them to check spam. Their booking is safe either way; the email is only a copy.

Someone wants a place in a full class

Open the class on the calendar and click + Add student. You are warned it is full and asked to confirm. It is recorded as your decision, so later you can see why that class went over its size.

I need to cancel tomorrow's class

Open it on the calendar, click Cancel class, and choose to email the students. Anyone who used a course session gets it back automatically.

Two students have the same name

Use their reference — MZ-0023 — shown next to every name.

A student booked twice by mistake

Open the class, find them on the list, and click Remove. Their course session is returned.

I want to stop taking online bookings for one class

Open it and raise Held back for chat bookings until the places on sale reach zero. The class stays on your calendar and stays visible, but nobody can book it online.

A course says "Not on sale yet"

Its shop product has not been linked. Open Settings and paste the product ID from WooCommerce → Products into that course's row — see section 11.

Nothing is arriving by email at all

Your dashboard will say so. Open Settings → Connected services — the reason is written there in plain words, with exactly what to change.
